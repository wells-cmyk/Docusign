# Signing rules: BC Contract of Purchase and Sale (BC2057, REV. DEC 2025)

Set by Wells, 2026-10-07. Apply to every CPS sent through DocuSign.

## Buyer initials
1. The buyer initials **every box labelled BUYER'S INITIALS**: the footer on every
   contract page, Section 22 (Acceptance Irrevocable / seal) and Section 23
   (Rescission disclosure).
2. Each initials group has three cells. **One buyer per cell**: buyer 1 → cell 1,
   buyer 2 → cell 2, buyer 3 → cell 3. A single buyer initials cell 1 only.
3. Agency boxes follow the contract. If only 21A (seller's agent) is filled in, the
   buyer initials 21D (unrepresented buyer). If 21B is filled in, the buyer initials 21B.
4. Section 26 citizenship (YES / NO) is a choice. The buyer initials one box, so both
   are placed as optional and the buyer picks.

## Cross-outs
5. Any printed text that is crossed out or edited needs a **buyer initial beside each
   change**. Never send a contract with a strike-through that has no initial tab.

## Box positions (PDF page, points from top-left)
See `cps-bc2057-tabs.json`. Footer groups: pages 3–9 at y=682, page 11 at y=635;
cells at x=27 / 66 / 105.
